Most of your pain isn’t from reality—it’s from your thoughts about what _might_ go wrong.  
You’ve fought battles that never happened. Lost things that were never yours.  
Worry makes you live a thousand lives—most of them filled with fear.  
But here's the truth: **You’ve survived every real moment so far.**
